我国热风干燥技术的应用研究进展

摘要：

阐述热风干燥技术的工作原理,介绍近年来我国热风干燥技术在农副产品(木材、食品、粮食、药材、种子)等加工领域的应用及研究现状,分析目前热风干燥技术存在的突出问题,并对其今后的应用发展趋势进行展望。

关键词： 热风干燥; 应用; 发展展望; 组合式干燥;
Abstract：

The principle of hot air drying technology is explained in this article. The author examines the application and research of hot air drying technology in the processing of agricultural and sideline products in recent years in China, and analyzes the major existent problems in this area and its future development prospect.

KeyWords： hot air drying; apply; development prospect; combined drying;
